About this notebook
Formality draws the typed expression stored in a Lean proof. Colors distinguish introductions, applications, recursors, and other expression categories. The conclusion sits at the bottom; formulas stay in the caption.
Lemma expansion and concrete induction operate on Lean expressions, and the transformed proof is submitted to Lean’s kernel. Axioms and any omitted subtrees are recorded. This is an expression tree, rather than a complete kernel inference derivation.
The browser uses a pinned Lean runtime and bundled libraries. Foundation’s Gödel figures are saved, checked exports; use the CLI to regenerate them.
